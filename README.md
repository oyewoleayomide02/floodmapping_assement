# floodmapping_assement
SPDC, on behalf of its Joint Venture partners, plans a gas development project in Okpokunou Field (OMLs 35 and 62) to boost domestic gas supply by 200 MMscf/day by 2029. Phase 1 involves developing 1.2 Tscf of gas and 24 MMstb of condensate. A baseline land use, land cover evaluation, and flood risk assessment are required as part of the EIA.
