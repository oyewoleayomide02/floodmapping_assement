# floodmapping_assement
The Shell Petroleum Development Company of Nigeria Limited (SPDC), on behalf of its Joint Venture partners, plans a gas development project in Okpokunou Field (OMLs 35 and 62) to boost domestic gas supply by 200 MMscf/day by 2029. Phase 1 involves developing 1.2 Tscf of gas and 24 MMstb of condensate. A baseline land use, land cover evaluation, and flood risk assessment are required as part of the EIA.

## INTRODUCTION:
Baseline land use and landcover evaluation and flood risk/susceptibility assessment study as part of the mandatory Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA). 
This presents the land use and landcover analysis as well as a static flood risk/susceptibility assessment of the project area based on a recent 2.7 meters (reference is Mean Sea Level) high water mark (in-situ measurement from a tide gauge) within the project area, using Remotely sensed optical satellite images, Digital Elevation Model (DEM) and geospatial technologies.

## STUDY OBJECTIVES:

The objectives of this study are:

•	To provide baseline land use and land cover for the project area.

•	Generate a flood susceptibility model of the project area.

•	Generate a flood impact model of the project area.

## CONCLUSIONS:
The study successfully deployed remote sensing and geospatial technologies to map baseline land use and landcover and generate a flood risk/susceptibility model. This study identified and presented these findings: This study identified the villages of **Amanagbene, Angala-An, Egbenegbene, Kalatuo Island, Obrigbene, Tamigbe, and Zideyeregbene, along with the Okpokunou 001 and Toumo 001 wells**, as flood-affected. Utilizing remote sensing and geospatial technologies, it mapped land use and generated a flood risk model based on a 2.7-meter high water mark from 2024. The flood risk/susceptibility and flood impact models generated for this study are static models, basic and sufficient for its purpose. More factors can still be integrated depending on the availability of required datasets.
